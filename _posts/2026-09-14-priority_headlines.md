- **Energy chokepoint squeeze going structural**: Houthi island seizure at the Red Sea entrance plus Saudi pipeline shutdowns plus Hormuz vessel strikes represent a coordinated multi-axis threat to global oil flows — Iran denying belligerency while proxies do the work is a classic plausible-deniability structure.
- **NATO border incursion threshold being tested**: Russian drones striking a passenger train Kyiv-to-Warsaw and hitting sites near the Polish border, with Lithuania scrambling jets — the alliance's response posture is being probed incrementally, and Poland-Ukraine border infrastructure (including arms supply lines) is now a contested target set.
- **CBRN coupling with conventional war**: Rosatom and Russian claims about strikes on Zaporozhye fuel convoys, alongside Ukraine's nuclear rhetoric, keep nuclear-safety incidents as a live escalation lever; data-center strikes add a digital-infrastructure targeting dimension.
- **Insider threat at physical border layer**: Chinese nationals in trusted CBP/DHS positions manipulating hardware at border stations is a distinct and concerning vector — physical supply-chain tampering inside government networks, distinct from prior IP-theft cases.
- **AI-for-weapons development confirmed**: Anthropic's disclosures of Russian kamikaze-drone software development and blocked bioweapons research validate prior AI-misuse warnings — expectation set for more state and non-state actors leveraging commercial AI.
- **Patch-gap weaponization becoming a doctrine**: BlueMoon's three-V8-zero-days-in-one-kit approach shows threat actors systematically scanning for the patch gap as a persistent attack surface.
- **Sabotage attribution volatility**: The French train derailment (suspected then ruled out) and the Bulgarian weapons-site fire (owner claims sabotage) — both underline the analytical hazard of rapid attribution in rail and energy incidents.
