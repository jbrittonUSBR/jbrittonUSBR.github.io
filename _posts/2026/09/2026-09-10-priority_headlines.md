## Emerging Patterns to Watch

- **Iran-US conflict crossing thresholds**: Mutual tanker strikes represent the largest wave of shipping attacks since the war began. Iran striking Jordan's Al-Azraq base — a U.S. military staging ground — signals willingness to target American force projection directly. Oil past $100 creates economic pressure multiplier.
- **AI IP theft as national security crisis**: The NSA/CISA/FBI joint advisory on Chinese model distillation is a significant escalation in official attribution. This is no longer a commercial dispute — it's being framed as intelligence collection against U.S. technological advantage.
- **Youth radicalization reaching inflection point**: Three independent EU sources all flagging the same problem — minors comprising one-third of terror arrests, online radicalization driving nihilistic violence. The Southport inquiry testimony suggests this is now a systemic, not anecdotal, threat.
- **German power grid attacks definitively attributed to domestic extremism**: Second arrest confirms climate-motivated sabotage, not Russian hybrid warfare. However, the pattern demonstrates how easily infrastructure attacks can generate misattribution risk during periods of geopolitical tension.
- **UK aviation outage as systemic vulnerability signal**: Two consecutive days of flight chaos from a single tech outage exposes fragility of centralized air traffic management systems — a potential template for what a coordinated cyberattack could achieve.
- **AI token theft bypassing MFA**: Infostealer logs containing replayable AI authentication tokens represent a new attack surface — AI agent infrastructure may be fundamentally less secure than traditional authentication models.
- **Russia-Ukraine entering simultaneous kinetic-cyber escalation**: Peace talks collapse coinciding with both military strikes on infrastructure and cyber operations against government systems suggests a deliberate Russian strategy to raise costs across all domains.
