# Strategic Overview

## 1. Houthi campaign against Saudi export infrastructure
- **Target selection has shifted from symbolic to systemic**: repeated strikes on the East-West pipeline, which bypasses Hormuz, plus Abqaiq and Riyadh airport, aim at export chokepoints and aviation rather than only military sites.
- **Market response is already decoupled from the attacks**: Brent fell 7% on Iran peace hopes despite a fresh Saudi pipeline attack, with OPEC output at 90% of pre-war levels — a signal that traders are pricing diplomacy over infrastructure risk.
- **Aviation spillover**: IndiGo extended its Saudi flight suspension to 30 November, and the US Embassy has warned of airport disruption, so commercial air access is becoming a secondary pressure point.

## 2. Russia–Ukraine energy war and the cyber dimension
- **Deep strike reach**: the Omsk refinery strike at roughly 3,000 km and the Baltic export-hub fire show Ukraine can now threaten both refining and export terminals, not just border-region assets.
- **Romania's Russian-linked cyber finding** extends the campaign into a NATO state's networks, reinforcing the pattern of kinetic and cyber pressure running in parallel.
- **Pipeline breaches affecting rail** (second day of train disruption) show how a single midstream failure propagates into transport.

## 3. Iran, refrigeration sabotage, and critical-infrastructure advisories
- **Attribution is now overt**: France's public naming of Iran and the summoning of its ambassador, alongside Iran's denial, moves the fridge campaign into state-level dispute.
- **The US advisory** to secure refrigeration systems is the first cross-sector control measure prompted by this campaign; it matters for grocery, cold chain, blood banks, and any facility with networked temperature control.
- **Scope of about 700 retail sites** puts this well beyond nuisance-level disruption.

## 4. Technology espionage and political escalation
- **Nvidia's Jensen Huang cluster continued** with calls for resignation, a possible congressional subpoena, and commentary on executive vulnerability, but no new operational fact about a compromised facility.
- **The US soldier arrested in the UK on Russian espionage allegations** is a concrete counterintelligence case and is carried above.
- **AI data-center demand** is now constraining fiber optic supply enough to affect Ukrainian drone operations, linking commercial buildout to battlefield capacity.

## 5. Water governance and urban resilience
- **Glen Canyon Dam's executive director advocated concentrating storage in Lake Mead and phasing out Lake Powell**, a long-horizon structural proposal rather than an incident.
- **Nebraska's Valentine dam** faces sediment threatening a fish production reservoir, another slow-moving asset decision.
- **Brooklyn's Flatbush Avenue collapse** remains the most consequential urban event in the batch, with eight dead, 18 injured, and inspections at about 100 construction sites.

- Whether Houthi strikes on the East-West pipeline produce confirmed throughput loss or force export rerouting.
- Escalation between Iran and France over the refrigeration campaign, including retaliatory cyber action against European retailers or health logistics.
- Further Ukrainian deep strikes on Russian refineries and Baltic export terminals, and Russian responses against Ukrainian grid assets.
- Restoration progress for Pacific Power, PG&E, and Entergy footprints, and any confirmation of tornado damage near ChristianaCare in Newark, DE.
