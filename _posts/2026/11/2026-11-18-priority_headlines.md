# Strategic Overview

## 1. The Iran dirty-bomb story is an intelligence claim inside a media spiral
- **The sourcing is thin and openly contested**: "very specific" reporting from the New York Post and Daily Mail ran alongside officials telling CNN the claims give "Iraq WMDs reporting," and a congressman noting the US receives such threats "quite often."
- **The political response outran the evidence**: Trump warned Iran would "pay a price no country has ever faced," while Tehran denied the plot and the same batch carried doomsday-prep features.
- **What it means**: the operative effect this cycle is rhetorical and posture-driven around Washington, DC — no confirmed device, location or timeline has surfaced.

## 2. Iran's external operations are broad, but this batch is claims and arrests
- **Gulf**: drones at the US base in Bahrain were intercepted, with Iran claiming a US radar as the intended target — a repeat of the pattern briefed on 17NOV.
- **Europe**: five arrests in Athens and Skopje over a planned assassination in Greece attributed to Iranian direction.
- **US and Israel**: a reported Mossad-to-CIA warning of a drone assassination plot against Trump, and an ISIS- and Hamas-inspired plot against the Temple Mount.

## 3. Houthi gains have become a Saudi command problem
- **Yemen's army reported 661 attacks on the Houthis** as the group advanced toward Aden (Al Jazeera English).
- **Riyadh dismissed the commander of its coalition forces** after the battlefield setback (Al Jazeera English; The Daily Caller) — the Bab el-Mandeb loss briefed yesterday has produced an accountability decision rather than a counteroffensive.

## 4. US continuity failures this batch were software and weather
- **The Chase/JPMorgan disruption was a software bug**, reported across three cards and explicitly cleared of cyberattack.
- **Down Detector clusters** hit UPS, Spectrum, EA, Stake, Microsoft and Outlook, with the FT reporting US tech outages blamed on a rush to fix AI-found bugs.
- **Weather drove the physical disruption**: flooding in Plantation, Florida and Oradell, New Jersey, plus storm damage in Veracruz.

## 5. Supply-chain compromise is the default cyber mode
- **Five vendor-chain items in one batch**: TSMC employee data leaked, Optimizely reaching 178 brands, Infoblox, Barracuda/ARTEX, and malicious code served through CNN, BBC and Zoom.
- **The pattern is third-party reach rather than targeted intrusion** — brand and customer exposure through vendors, with no industrial control-system hit reported.

## 6. Maritime drone risk spread beyond Ukraine's ports
- **A Greek-owned vessel, Gas Husky, sank in the Black Sea** amid reports of a drone attack, and a cargo ship capsized off Bulgaria after a reported sea drone attack.
- **Russia's FSB said a Ukrainian naval drone attack on Russian ships in the Caspian Sea was thwarted**, extending the naval-drone map well east of the Black Sea.
