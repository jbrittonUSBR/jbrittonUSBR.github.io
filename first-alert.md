# CI SECTOR BRIEF

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Bottom line, Dataminr reporting indicates a broad critical infrastructure operating picture across North America marked by utility outages, hazardous materials and gas incidents, cyber related service disruption and restoration, and protest activity near government and diplomatic sites, with all reporting at URGENT or ALERT priority and the most immediate significance centered on active public safety hazards and service continuity impacts.

## DETAIL

Public safety and infrastructure hazard reporting was led by an overturned tanker truck containing liquid hydrogen in Bellingham, Washington that prompted evacuation and a power shutoff near Taylor Avenue and 30th Street, a possible utility main line hazard near Canyon Pines in Arvada, Colorado that prompted evacuations, and a possible gas leak at the University of Washington Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering in Seattle that later gave way to reopening of the Electrical and Computer Engineering building⁴²˒¹˒²˒³. Additional hazardous incident reporting included one person hospitalized due to a suspected carbon monoxide leak inside a semi truck on Collings Drive in Columbus, Ohio, an electrical vault explosion at Westchester Park in Los Angeles reportedly affecting thousands of residences and dozens of businesses through a power outage, and a propane tank explosion in Smiths Falls, Ontario that left two motorhomes and one boat on fire¹³˒²¹˒⁴¹.

Utility and energy disruptions were geographically dispersed, with power disruption reported in Appalachia, Virginia during a storm, a power outage reported in several areas of Yucatan, Mexico, and multiple outage trackers showing elevated customer impacts in Georgia, South Carolina, and Florida⁴˒¹⁷˒¹⁸˒¹⁹˒²⁰˒²³˒²⁴˒²⁵˒²⁶˒²⁷˒²⁸. In Georgia, Cobb EMC outages rose above 18,500 in Cobb and above 19,000 across two counties before later dropping below 12,000 and 12,500 respectively, while Sawnee Electric outages exceeded 11,500 across two counties and more than 10,500 in Fulton before later dropping below 10,500²⁶˒²⁷˒³⁶˒³⁷˒¹⁸˒¹⁹˒²⁰. Separate outage trackers showed more than 10,000 Georgia Power customers affected in Gwinnett County, more than 10,000 FPL customers affected in Miami Dade, and Palmetto Electric outages rising above 14,500 in Bluffton and above 16,000 across two South Carolina counties²⁵˒²⁴˒²²˒²³. In Mexico, the Federal Electricity Commission launched preventive deployment and contingency measures tied to Hurricane Polo and possible restoration work along the coasts of Guerrero, Michoacan, Jalisco, and Colima¹⁴˒²⁹.

Cyber and communications reporting showed mixed degradation and recovery across service providers and platforms. Direct Communications in Eagle Mountain, Utah confirmed a DDoS attack disrupting internet and phone services, later said the attack continued to disrupt service, and subsequently local reporting said internet service had been restored for customers³⁹˒³⁸˒¹⁰. Separately, E-911 service in Atlanta was reported back up after a technical outage, airport operations in the Northeast were said to be resuming after telecom lines were repaired, and reporting from Newark indicated up to 36 of 56 fiber cables had been repaired with others expected online shortly¹²˒³⁴˒³². Platform and enterprise service issues also appeared in sensor and corporate reporting, with Robinhood, Reddit, and Concur experiencing outages in the United States, while Coinbase said an outage impacting online services had been resolved³⁸˒³⁵˒⁴⁰˒³³.

Industrial and energy sector reporting in Mexico and Canada pointed to lingering operational strain. In Veracruz, reporting said evaporation of gases from a damaged salt plant in Jaltipan prompted ammonia odor in Acayucan and Soconusco, while separate local reporting described an ammonia odor following a recent fire at a salt plant in Jaltipan de Morelos¹⁶˒¹⁷. In New Brunswick, Point Lepreau nuclear power plant was reported still offline due to a radioactive heavy water leak, with no indication on when operations would resume³⁰. Longer horizon energy development reporting also noted South Korea's approval of construction of a gas fired power plant in Encinal, Texas, described as the first project under a $200 billion US investment initiative and as a project with 50 percent ownership shared with the US²⁸˒²⁹.

Protest activity clustered around government and diplomatic locations in New York, Austin, and Ottawa. In New York, protesters were reported blocking the intersection of 2nd Avenue and East 43rd Street, with arrests by NYPD at the same location, while later reporting described a large protest mentioning Iran at Dag Hammarskjold Plaza and a protest by Venezuelan nationals against Interim Venezuelan President Rodriguez's visit to the United States for the UN General Assembly at the same plaza⁸˒⁹˒⁶˒⁷. In Austin, multiple reports described a protest mentioning US ICE blocking the area at or outside the Texas Capitol, including one report that protesters held signs against US ICE²˒³¹˒⁵˒³². In Ottawa, protesters were reported arrested on Parliament Hill¹¹.

Political and security adjacent reporting included a statement attributed to US Secretary of State Rubio that CNN and MSNBC were not being allowed to work from the White House, alongside separate reporting that major US media networks cut live coverage of US President Trump, including the opening of a new helipad at the White House and a trip to the UN General Assembly¹¹˒³¹. A separate alert reported an individual posting an image of a firearm pointed at Chinese President Xi Jinping and making violent threats tied to a possible US visit³⁰. Another alert captured a ShinyHunters branded extortion group message directed at the Clop ransomware group³¹.

## IMPACT

The operating picture suggests concurrent but mostly localized stress across several critical infrastructure categories, with the clearest near term operational consequences tied to hazardous materials incidents, gas related evacuations, and electric service interruptions affecting public access, business continuity, and emergency response posture.¹˒²˒⁴˒¹³˒¹⁸˒²²˒²³˒²⁴˒²⁵˒²⁶˒²⁷˒⁴² The Washington state incidents are especially relevant because they combined evacuation orders, power shutoff, and later controlled reopening, indicating that some hazards were actively managed but still capable of causing short duration disruption to transportation, campus operations, and nearby utilities.²˒³˒⁴² The Georgia, South Carolina, Florida, Virginia, California, and Yucatan outage reporting indicates a dispersed reliability challenge rather than a single common cause, with some areas showing restoration progress while others were still escalating at the time of reporting.⁴˒¹⁸˒¹⁹˒²⁰˒²²˒²³˒²⁴˒²⁵˒²⁶˒²⁷

The Utah communications incident is notable because the provider explicitly attributed service disruption to a DDoS attack and later reporting indicated restoration, which supports decisions around validating redundancy for internet and voice dependent operations and checking whether any residual instability remains for customers in Eagle Mountain.³⁸˒³⁹˒¹⁰ The Atlanta E-911 recovery and Northeast airport telecom repair reporting indicate that some high consequence communications disruptions were moving back toward normal operations, but they also highlight the dependence of emergency and transportation systems on telecom resilience.¹²˒³²˒³⁴

The protest reporting supports a heightened access and mobility risk picture around symbolic government and diplomatic sites, particularly near the UN area in Manhattan and the Texas Capitol, where road blockage and arrests were reported.⁵˒⁶˒⁷˒⁸˒⁹˒¹¹ These events are likely to matter most for protective security, route planning, staffing access, and event security coordination rather than for sustained infrastructure damage. The Mexico hurricane preparation alerts also suggest utilities are positioning for possible wider disruption if storm effects intensify along the Pacific coast.¹⁴˒²⁹

Outlook will depend on whether outage counts continue to decline in the southeastern United States, whether any renewed evacuation or shelter in place messaging emerges from Washington or Colorado hazard sites, and whether restored telecom and internet services remain stable. Watch for fresh utility tracker spikes, official extension of road closures around protest locations, additional provider statements on cyber related service degradation, and any escalation from precautionary utility deployments in western Mexico to confirmed storm damage and restoration operations.
