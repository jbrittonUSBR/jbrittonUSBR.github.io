# CI Sector Brief 2026-09-25

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Bottom line, Dataminr alerts indicate a broad critical infrastructure operating picture across North America on 24 to 25 September, led by multiple utility and transit disruptions, hazardous materials and fire incidents, and protest related transport impacts, with URGENT level reporting concentrated on power, telecom, and public safety disruptions in the United States, Canada, and Mexico. The immediate significance is a dispersed but active environment in which localized outages and hazardous incidents are affecting mobility, public services, and utility continuity, while several events show signs of stabilization or restoration.

## DETAIL

Power and utility disruptions were reported across several jurisdictions during the period. In Oregon, the number of Portland General Electric customers affected by a power outage across five counties increased to more than 11,500¹. In Ontario, approximately 16,000 customers were reported without power in Huron and Perth counties due to an unknown cause, and separate reporting later put the Hydro One impact at 15,000 customers in parts of the same area²˒³. In New Mexico, a power outage was reported in the Lincoln area while flooding continued near US 70 in Hondo⁴. In Massachusetts, a gas leak prompted evacuation near the Holyoke Gas & Electric Department, and later reporting said the leak had been contained⁵˒⁶. In Idaho, a propane leak was reported on the 800 block of 4th Street in Priest River⁷.

Several fire and explosion related incidents affected infrastructure and public safety. In Palmerton, Pennsylvania, initial reporting described a fire involving multiple mobile homes at Sunny Rest Resort with a possible propane tank explosion⁸. Follow on reporting said a rapid intervention team was dispatched and multiple water tankers were requested because of the fire and possible propane tank explosion⁹. Later reporting said no one was reported entrapped during the fire, which also involved propane tanks exploding¹⁰. In Woodridge, Illinois, three people were hospitalized and two firefighters were treated on scene after a fire during a gas meter installation at Windsor Lakes Apartment¹¹. In Santa Barbara, California, a transformer fire prompted a power outage for several downtown blocks near East Ortega Street and Anacapa Street¹². In Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario, a gas explosion at a building prompted evacuation on Gore Street¹³. In Tepeaca, Puebla, four gas tanks exploded, injuring three people and prompting the evacuation of more than 2,000 people¹⁴.

Transit and transport disruptions were also reported. At Revolucion metro station in Mexico City, initial eyewitness reporting described a possible explosion with smoke rising and passengers evacuating¹⁵. Additional reporting said smoke impacted the platform due to a reported explosion, and passengers evacuated as smoke affected the station¹⁶˒¹⁷. Later reporting said service on Line 2 at Revolucion station was restored after an umbrella fell onto the tracks and caused smoke¹⁸. In Honolulu, multiple people reportedly felt sick after an unknown odor on a flight arriving at Daniel K Inouye Airport, including seven evaluated on scene¹⁹. In Norman, Oklahoma, police reported that 911 service was fully restored²⁰.

Digital service disruptions were mixed, with one issue ongoing and another resolved. Sensor reporting indicated Cox was experiencing an outage in the United States²¹. GitLab said it was investigating a service disruption affecting GitLab[.]com, and later said the issue had been resolved²²˒²³. Sensor reporting also indicated Snapchat was experiencing an outage in the United States²⁴.

In Mexico, infrastructure stress and governance related activity extended beyond single point incidents. Reporting from Ciudad Juarez said flooding and power outages damaged a total of 14 schools²⁵. Separate reporting from Ciudad Juarez said the Federal Municipal Sanitation Authority evicted employees and raided municipal sanitation headquarters²⁶. In Tabasco, local reporting said gas leaks affected at least 150,000 people across 40 communities in the municipalities of Teapa, Jalapa, and Centro²⁷. Additional reporting from Tabasco said PRI state leader Barrueta called for action over CFE outages, water shortages, and odors from Campo Agave flares²⁸.

Protest activity created recurring transport and public order disruptions, especially in New York City. Near the UN building, protesters blocked roads and portions of 1st Avenue near East 39th Street, with reporting also citing at least 100 police officers on scene²⁹˒³⁰˒³¹. NYPD arrests began early in the demonstration, with later reporting describing several dozen arrests, named individuals among those arrested, and local news reporting that over 100 people were arrested during the demonstration against Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu³²˒³³˒³⁴˒³⁵˒³⁶˒³⁷˒³⁸. Protest activity later spread through Midtown, including Grand Central Terminal, East 42nd Street, 3rd Avenue, Dag Hammarskjold Plaza, the Theater District, West 47th Street, and Times Square, where reporting described blocked roads, police cordons, a reported lockdown at Grand Central Terminal, confrontations with counter protesters, a burning effigy of Netanyahu, one arrest during confrontations at 2nd Avenue and East 46th Street, and eventual dispersal at Times Square with no reported arrests there³⁹˒⁴⁰˒⁴¹˒⁴²˒⁴³˒⁴⁴˒⁴⁵˒⁴⁶˒⁴⁷˒⁴⁸˒⁴⁹˒⁵⁰˒⁵¹˒⁵²˒⁵³. In Washington, DC, one protest mentioning Tibet was reported moving toward the US Capitol, while separate protest activity near the White House involved a fight among protesters, additional DC Police and US Secret Service response, several arrests following clashes, and later dispersal with a heavy police presence⁵⁴˒⁵⁵˒⁵⁶˒⁵⁷. In San Francisco, a protest blocked the area outside the Chinese Consulate General⁵⁸.

Other notable governance and sector relevant developments were also reported. In Texas, Attorney General Paxton said he opened an investigation into hundreds of data center developments in the state to ensure compliance with water use reporting requirements⁵⁹. Ukrainian Energy Minister Shmyhal said more than 40 countries had joined the Energy Ramstein initiative and provided $545 million, with another EUR 305 million in confirmed commitments to support Ukraine's energy sector⁶⁰. In Washington, DC, reporting also said ABC News journalists were reportedly refusing to enter White House events, and separate reporting said ABC News journalists were refusing to film until CNN, MSNOW, and Politico staffers were allowed back inside the White House⁶¹˒⁶². In New York City, three police officers experienced symptoms and one Secret Service member was reportedly transported from the Plaza Hotel due to possible foodborne illness⁶³.

## IMPACT

The reporting points to a fragmented but elevated operating environment for critical infrastructure stakeholders, with the most immediate operational pressure coming from electric service interruptions, hazardous gas and fire incidents, and protest driven mobility constraints. Multiple reports indicate that utility and municipal responders were managing simultaneous localized disruptions rather than a single regional failure pattern¹˒²˒³˒⁵˒⁶˒⁸˒⁹˒¹⁰.

For electric and gas operators, the incidents in Oregon, Ontario, Holyoke, Priest River, Tepeaca, and Tabasco highlight continued exposure to both routine outage risk and higher consequence fuel related events that can quickly expand into evacuations, injuries, or broad public concern¹˒²˒³˒⁵˒⁶˒⁷˒¹⁴˒²⁷˒²⁸. The Palmerton, Woodridge, Santa Barbara, and Sault Ste. Marie incidents also show how localized equipment or fuel events can create immediate downstream effects on housing, commercial areas, and emergency resource demand¹¹˒¹²˒¹³.

For transportation and public venue operators, the Mexico City metro incident and the Honolulu flight odor event underscore how ambiguous initial reporting can trigger evacuations, medical evaluations, and service interruptions before a clearer cause is established¹⁵˒¹⁶˒¹⁷˒¹⁸˒¹⁹. The later restoration of Line 2 service in Mexico City suggests some incidents may resolve quickly once the source is identified, but the initial disruption still carries crowd management and reputational implications¹⁸.

For communications and digital service stakeholders, the Cox and Snapchat outage reports indicate continuing sensitivity to consumer facing service degradation, while the GitLab sequence shows the value of rapid corporate acknowledgment and closure messaging during platform disruptions²¹˒²²˒²³˒²⁴. This supports decisions around customer communications tempo, escalation thresholds, and whether to posture for secondary impacts on dependent users.

The New York protest sequence is significant because it combined sustained road blockages, repeated police intervention, arrests, and movement across multiple high traffic corridors and transit adjacent locations over many hours²⁹˒³⁰˒³¹˒³²˒³³˒³⁴˒³⁵˒³⁹˒⁴⁰˒⁴¹˒⁴²˒⁴³˒⁴⁴˒⁴⁵˒⁴⁶˒⁴⁷˒⁴⁸˒⁴⁹˒⁵⁰˒⁵¹˒⁵²˒⁵³. That pattern supports decisions to review staffing, access control, route planning, and contingency transport options for facilities in Midtown Manhattan and near diplomatic or government sites. The Washington protest reporting suggests a lower scale but still relevant risk of rapid escalation near protected federal areas⁵⁴˒⁵⁵˒⁵⁶˒⁵⁷.

The broader picture suggests that many incidents remained localized and some were already stabilizing by the end of the reporting window, but the volume and diversity of disruptions increase the chance of compounding operational friction for organizations with distributed footprints. It is likely that the near term risk is less about one dominant crisis and more about concurrent short duration disruptions that strain response coordination and business continuity.

Outlook should focus on whether outage counts continue rising or begin to normalize, whether any of the gas and fire incidents produce wider evacuations or prolonged service loss, and whether protest activity reconstitutes around major transport nodes or government sites. Additional official restoration notices, casualty updates, evacuation expansions, or renewed road closure reporting would confirm whether the current trajectory is easing or intensifying.
