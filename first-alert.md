# CI Sector Brief 2026-09-23

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Bottom line, Dataminr alerts indicate a broad critical infrastructure operating picture dominated by multiple power disruptions across the United States and Mexico, a battery storage fire and related protective actions in La Verne, California, several hazardous materials and gas leak incidents affecting schools and campuses, and a smaller set of cyber and protest developments across North America. The reporting is primarily URGENT level and points to near term operational relevance for utilities, education facilities, transport safety, and public order monitoring across several jurisdictions.¹

## DETAIL

The most sustained infrastructure incident in the reporting centers on La Verne, California, where a battery storage fire prompted evacuations, a shelter in place order at Grace Miller Elementary School, and later a partial lifting of evacuations for all areas except between Moreno Avenue and B Street South.¹˒²˒³ The incident was tied to a lithium ion battery fire at Metropolitan Water District on Wheeler Avenue, while government reporting stated there was no impact to water safety after the fire near the Weymouth Water Treatment Plant.²˒⁴˒⁵ A separate follow on report also states that an investigative report into the June 17 fire on Los Palos Street in Los Angeles confirmed, according to a Lineage spokesperson, that "this is a solar fire."⁶

Power disruption reporting was geographically dispersed and significant in scale. In Idaho, outage reporting showed Idaho Power customers impacted in Eagle rising to over 13,000, followed by local reporting that more than 23,000 customers in Eagle and Star were without power.⁷˒⁸ In Texas, CenterPoint Energy reported more than 14,500 customers affected in Magnolia, with a later update indicating the number had dropped below 14,500, while separate local reporting said more than 7,000 El Paso Electric customers in El Paso and parts of southern New Mexico were without power.⁹˒¹⁰˒¹¹ In Mexico, local reporting cited a large fire at a Federal Electricity Commission substation in the Silao area of Guanajuato as causing outages, with later reporting describing fire and explosion impacts that affected sectors of Irapuato and Silao, while another report said around 70,000 residents were affected by a power outage in Acolman and additional outages were reported in several areas of Yucatan.¹²˒¹³˒¹⁴˒¹⁵ Flood related utility strain was also noted in Guerrero, where authorities warned of rising river levels in Petatlan and Zihuatanejo, twenty two temporary shelters were prepared, and water outages were reported.¹⁶

Several hazardous materials and gas related incidents affected educational and custodial facilities. Near Blue Ridge Community College in Weyers Cave, Virginia, a hazardous material incident prompted a shelter in place order, then an early campus closure, before the shelter in place was lifted and the college remained closed until Wednesday.¹⁷˒¹⁸˒¹⁹ In Seattle, the University of Washington reported a possible gas leak at its Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering building, followed later by notice that the building was reopening.²⁰˒²¹ In Aspen Hill, Maryland, an antifreeze leak on an occupied school bus prompted a hazmat response and evacuation of the vehicle, with no serious injuries reported.²² In Eureka, Kansas, a gas leak prompted evacuation at Greenwood County Jail.²³ In Louisville, Kentucky, more than 20 students were hospitalized after falling ill at Pleasure Ridge Park High School on September 21, though crews reportedly found no indication of a gas leak or other environmental hazard within the building.²⁴

Cyber and service disruption reporting was limited but operationally relevant. Burnaby School District in British Columbia confirmed an active investigation into a cyber incident on September 21 that disrupted operations, including internal network services and phone lines.²⁵ In Mexico, a database allegedly linked to Agua de Puebla containing information of nearly 800,000 users was allegedly put up for sale on the Deep Web.²⁶ In Washington, DC, the FBI said it is investigating claims regarding unauthorized activity affecting its jobs site.²⁷ Separately, outage sensors indicated Netflix service disruptions in the United States, Brazil, and the United Kingdom.²⁸˒²⁹˒³⁰

Public order reporting showed several protest actions near symbolic or high visibility sites. In New York City, a large protest mentioning Iran was reported at Dag Hammarskjold Plaza near the UN building, while separate local reporting said Venezuelan nationals protested there against Interim Venezuelan President Rodriguez's visit to the United States for the UN General Assembly.³¹˒³² Later in lower Manhattan, protesters blocked the entrance to One World Trade Center on Fulton Street.³³ In Ottawa, protesters were arrested on Parliament Hill.³⁴

A lower tempo governance item from Mexico indicated that Xalapa city officials and residents agreed to conduct technical studies to improve drinking water and sewer systems and address runoff and street conditions.³⁵

## IMPACT

The reporting points to a dispersed but meaningful strain on infrastructure operations, with the clearest concentration in electric power disruptions and fire related utility risk across parts of the United States and Mexico.⁷˒⁸˒⁹˒¹⁰˒¹¹˒¹²˒¹³˒¹⁴˒¹⁵ The La Verne incident is especially relevant because it combined fire, evacuation, school protective measures, and concern around nearby water infrastructure, even though government messaging said water safety was not affected.¹˒²˒³˒⁴˒⁵

For operators, the immediate decisions supported by this reporting include prioritizing continuity measures for utilities and public facilities in outage affected areas, validating protective actions around schools and campuses during hazmat or gas incidents, and maintaining public messaging where secondary effects such as water safety concerns could drive confusion.¹⁶˒¹⁷˒¹⁸˒¹⁹˒²⁰˒²¹˒²²˒²³ Multiple reports also indicate that cyber related disruptions remain capable of degrading basic institutional functions such as phone lines, internal networks, and public facing employment systems, even where the full scope is still unclear.²⁵˒²⁶˒²⁷

The protest activity does not by itself indicate a broad escalation trend, but it does show recurring mobilization near high visibility sites in New York and Ottawa that could complicate access, security posture, or event operations on short notice.³¹˒³²˒³³˒³⁴ The Netflix outage reports appear more relevant as a cross regional service disruption signal than as a direct critical infrastructure issue, though they may still inform broader monitoring of digital service reliability.²⁸˒²⁹˒³⁰

Outlook remains mixed, with no single unifying cause across the alert set. Key indicators to watch are whether the La Verne fire area sees renewed evacuation zones or new water quality warnings, whether the larger power outages in Idaho, Texas, and Mexico expand or stabilize, and whether the Burnaby, Agua de Puebla, or FBI related cyber reporting develops into confirmed compromise details or wider service degradation.
