# CI Sector Brief 2026-09-23

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Bottom line up front: Dataminr alerts indicate a broad set of lower priority incidents across the United States, Canada, and Mexico, with the most operationally significant developments involving hazardous materials and gas related evacuations, a multi jurisdiction 911 disruption in Oklahoma, and protest activity affecting transportation and security perimeters in New York, Montreal, and Joint Base Andrews. Immediate significance centers on localized life safety risk, temporary communications degradation, and short duration transport and access disruptions rather than a single continent wide driver.

## DETAIL

In Oklahoma, multiple jurisdictions reported 911 disruptions tied to an AT&T outage, with emergency management offices across the state, including Duncan and Chickasha, citing impacts to 911 systems, Seminole County 911 warning that dispatches statewide were affected, and local police in Chickasha, Purcell, and Norman issuing alternative contact guidance or outage notices³⁰˒³¹˒³²˒³³˒³⁴. Norman Police later reported that 911 service was fully restored, indicating at least partial recovery within the statewide disruption window²⁹.

Hazardous materials and gas incidents drove several evacuations and sheltering actions. In East Bakersfield, authorities issued a shelter in place order tied to a hazardous materials release, with local reporting specifying a hydrochloric acid leak on Virginia Avenue and a hazmat response affecting zone KRN-421¹˒²˒³. In West Covina, a semi truck hazardous materials leak shut the eastbound 10 Freeway and the Pacific Avenue off ramp, causing heavy traffic backups⁴. Additional gas leak evacuations were reported near Holyoke Gas and Electric in Holyoke, near South Iowa Avenue and West Main Street in Washington, Iowa, and at schools and nearby buildings in Tlaquepaque, Jalisco, where one incident later was reported under control after evacuating 540 students⁵˒⁶˒⁷˒⁸. In Piedras Negras, Coahuila, two reports described an explosion and fire following a gas leak at a residence, with four people hospitalized, including children⁹˒¹⁰.

Separate infrastructure and utility disruptions were also reported. SDG&E outage data showed more than 19,000 customers affected in Oceanside and more than 19,500 customers impacted across two California counties¹¹˒¹². Hydro Quebec reported outages affecting more than 10,000 customers across 54 regions in Canada¹³. A scanner source reported a power outage at the Long Island Railroad in Queens²⁴. In Nova Scotia, post tropical storm Fiona reportedly caused significant damage, massive power outages, and unstable cellphone service province wide¹⁴. In Ciudad Juarez, 10 wells were reported out of service after an electrical storm, while separate reporting said more than 400 million liters of water had been captured in the Sierra de Juarez area¹⁵˒¹⁶. In Guerrero, the Mexican National Water Commission issued drought alerts for 83 of 85 municipalities¹⁷.

Platform and communications disruptions were limited but notable. Down Detector sensors indicated outages affecting EA in the United States and Snapchat in the US¹⁸˒³⁵.

Protest activity produced several transportation and security effects. In New York City, a demonstration blocked part of West Street near One World Trade Center and later reporting said several protesters were arrested as the action, which mentioned Palestinian territories, blocked part of the West Side Highway²⁵˒²⁶. Also in New York, protests against Guinean President Doumbouya near the Waldorf Astoria prompted traffic delays, and reporter video described protesters chasing a van and throwing bottles before police responded²⁷˒²⁸. Another protest in New York involved immigrants confronting Georgian Foreign Minister Maka Bochorishvili and ministry officials outside a restaurant³⁷. In Montreal, protests outside the Quebec election party leaders debate at Radio Canada blocked the area, multiple arrests were reported, and counter protesters were seen attempting to seize a banner from a protest group²⁰˒²¹˒²². At Joint Base Andrews, clashes between protesters and counter protesters prompted a large police response and road closure outside the main gate, after which protesters and supporters dispersed and Secret Service agents arrived, followed later by SWAT arriving as President Trump departed aboard Marine One²³˒²⁴˒³⁶.

Other localized public safety incidents included the evacuation of all occupants from a commercial structure after multiple explosions in West Jordan, Utah¹⁹, the evacuation of dozens of residents from two Milwaukee apartment buildings because of electrical and fire alarm system issues²³, multiple hospitalizations after a broken thermometer at a Chattanooga middle school¹⁴, and a reported gas tank explosion in Tapachula, Chiapas³⁷.

## IMPACT

The most consequential operational shift in this reporting was the temporary degradation of emergency call handling across parts of Oklahoma, because multiple local agencies indicated 911 access problems at the same time and some had to publish alternate emergency numbers³⁰˒³¹˒³²˒³³˒³⁴. The later restoration notice from Norman suggests the disruption may have been transient in some areas, but the reporting does not establish full statewide recovery²⁹.

The Bakersfield hazardous materials incident stands out because it combined official sheltering guidance with local reporting that identified hydrochloric acid and a defined impact zone, which supports immediate protective action and route planning around East Bakersfield¹˒²˒³. The West Covina freeway hazmat leak and the cluster of gas leak evacuations in Massachusetts, Iowa, Jalisco, and Coahuila indicate a wider pattern of localized industrial or utility related safety events that can quickly create perimeter controls, school or building evacuations, and traffic disruption⁴˒⁵˒⁶˒⁷˒⁸˒⁹˒¹⁰.

Power and utility reporting points to uneven but meaningful service stress rather than a single interconnected grid event. The California, Quebec, Nova Scotia, and Ciudad Juarez items suggest that operators may need to account for localized outages, degraded communications, and water service effects when planning continuity or field movement¹¹˒¹²˒¹³˒¹⁴˒¹⁵˒¹⁶. The drought alert in Guerrero adds a slower moving resource pressure signal rather than an acute disruption¹⁷.

The protest reporting supports decisions around route avoidance, venue security, and executive movement monitoring in New York, Montreal, and Joint Base Andrews²⁰˒²¹˒²²˒²³˒²⁴˒²⁵˒²⁶˒²⁷˒²⁸˒³⁶˒³⁷. One alert also contains violent rhetoric tied to a claimed travel window for Israeli Prime Minister Netanyahu in New Jersey, but the reporting is a single sourced statement and does not confirm itinerary details, so it is best treated as a potential security concern requiring corroboration rather than a verified threat³⁶.

It is likely that near term disruption will remain localized and episodic, with the highest practical risk concentrated in emergency communications outages, hazmat perimeters, and protest related traffic interference. Multiple reports indicate some incidents were already stabilizing, but several utility and public safety events remained active at the time of reporting.

Watch for additional official notices confirming broader 911 restoration across Oklahoma, expansion or lifting of shelter in place and evacuation zones in Bakersfield and other gas leak sites, and utility updates showing whether customer outage counts are rising or falling. Further arrests, road closures, or protective security deployments around high visibility political venues or transit corridors would confirm continued protest escalation, while reopening of roads and dispersal of crowds would refute that trajectory.
